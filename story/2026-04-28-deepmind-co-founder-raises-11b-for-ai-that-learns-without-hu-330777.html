DeepMind co-founder raises $1.1B for AI that learns without human data

David Silver, a co-founder of DeepMind, has secured $1.1 billion in funding for his new AI lab, Ineffable Intelligence, which aims to develop AI systems that learn without relying on human-generated data.

Why this matters

This massive investment in "Ineffable Intelligence" signals a significant push towards foundational AI research that aims to overcome the limitations and biases of human-curated datasets. If successful, this approach could lead to more robust, general-purpose AI systems and reduce the reliance on vast, expensive human data collection.
