Models & Research

  1. ChatGPT's new health upgrade with GPT-5.5 Instant reportedly beats doctor-written answers

    OpenAI claims that its upgraded ChatGPT, powered by GPT-5.5 Instant, now outperforms human doctors in accuracy, clarity, and completeness for health and wellness responses, with a 71% reduction in health-related error rates.

    The Decoder·the-decoder.com ↗

  2. OpenAI introduces LifeSciBench, a 750-task benchmark for AI in life science research

    OpenAI has launched LifeSciBench, an expert-authored and reviewed benchmark with 750 tasks across seven workflows and biological domains, designed to evaluate AI systems on real-world life science research and decision-making.

    Marktechpost·marktechpost.com ↗

  3. GLM-5.2 released as a powerful 753B parameter open-weights LLM for long-horizon tasks

    Chinese AI lab Z.ai has released GLM-5.2, a 753-billion parameter Mixture of Experts (MoE) model, under an MIT license, touted as a highly capable text-only open-weights LLM for long-horizon tasks.

    Simon Willison·simonwillison.net ↗

Industry & Business

  1. SpaceX to acquire AI coding platform Cursor for $60 billion in major market move

    SpaceX is set to acquire the AI coding platform Cursor for $60 billion, a move aimed at enhancing its AI capabilities and positioning itself as a stronger competitor against leading AI companies like Anthropic and OpenAI.

    Ars Technica AI·arstechnica.com ↗

  2. OpenAI hires Transformer co-inventor Noam Shazeer and former Trump AI policy official Dean Ball

    OpenAI is bolstering its leadership team by hiring Noam Shazeer, co-inventor of the Transformer architecture and former Google Gemini co-lead, along with Dean Ball, a former Trump administration AI policy official, ahead of its anticipated IPO.

    TechCrunch AI·techcrunch.com ↗

  3. AI inference startup Baseten reportedly raising $1.5B at $13B valuation

    AI inference startup Baseten is reportedly in the final stages of securing a $1.5 billion funding round, which would value the company at $13 billion, signaling continued strong investor interest in the 'inference gold rush'.

    TechCrunch AI·techcrunch.com ↗

Tools & Code

  1. Perplexity launches 'Brain,' a self-improving memory system for its Computer agent

    Perplexity has introduced 'Brain,' a new self-improving memory system for its Computer agent that records and learns from the agent's work, building a traceable context graph and reporting overnight gains in correctness, recall, and cost efficiency.

    Marktechpost·marktechpost.com ↗

  2. arXiv paper proposes Decoupled Search Grounding for LLM agents to separate search from reasoning

    A new paper introduces Decoupled Search Grounding (DSG), a vendor-agnostic architecture that externalizes real-time search from LLM agents' reasoning models, aiming to improve inspectability, tuning, and reusability of grounding processes.

    arXiv cs.AI·arxiv.org ↗

  3. arXiv paper advocates for an 'Agent-First Web' redesign to better serve AI agents

    A research paper proposes a fundamental redesign of the World Wide Web towards an 'Agent-First Web,' acknowledging AI agents as primary consumers of content and advocating for changes in access models, economics, and content targeting.

    arXiv cs.AI·arxiv.org ↗

Policy & Safety

  1. Alleged China ties at SK Telecom alarmed US officials, triggering Anthropic's Fable crisis

    US officials reportedly raised concerns over alleged ties between South Korean conglomerate SK Telecom and China, leading to the White House intervening and restricting SK Telecom's access to Anthropic's Claude Mythos AI model through Project Glasswing.

    The Decoder·the-decoder.com ↗

  2. Bernie Sanders unveils $7 trillion plan to give Americans control of AI industry

    Senator Bernie Sanders has introduced a $7 trillion proposal aimed at establishing an AI wealth fund to grant Americans collective ownership and control over the rapidly growing artificial intelligence industry.

    Ars Technica AI·arstechnica.com ↗

  3. AI data centers get government-mandated fast lane to grid, raising energy supply concerns

    The Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC) has directed grid operators to prioritize interconnection requests for AI data centers, granting them a fast lane to the electricity grid, but without addressing potential electricity supply shortages.

    TechCrunch AI·techcrunch.com ↗
