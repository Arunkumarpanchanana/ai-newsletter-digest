  1. Google DeepMind's Gemma 4 12B Multimodal AI Model Runs Locally on Laptops with 16GB RAM

    Google DeepMind has released Gemma 4 12B, an open-source multimodal AI model capable of processing text, images, and audio natively, and designed to run efficiently on laptops with just 16 GB of RAM under an Apache 2.0 license.

  2. Microsoft Unveils New MAI-Thinking-1 and MAI-Code-1-Flash LLMs at Build Conference

    Microsoft announced two new in-house large language models, MAI-Thinking-1 for advanced reasoning and MAI-Code-1-Flash optimized for GitHub Copilot and VS Code, signaling a strategic push in foundational AI capabilities.

    Simon Willison·simonwillison.net ↗

  3. MiniMax M3 Model Features 1M-Token Context, Native Multimodality, and Agentic Coding

    MiniMax has launched its M3 model, incorporating a MiniMax Sparse Attention (MSA) architecture that supports a 1M-token context window, native image and video processing, and advanced agentic coding capabilities.

  1. Alphabet Secures Record-Breaking $85 Billion Investment for Google's AI Business

    TechCrunch AI·techcrunch.com ↗

  2. Uber Caps Employee AI Spending After Exceeding Budget in Four Months

    Uber has implemented caps on employee usage of AI tools, including Claude Code, after exhausting its annual AI budget within the first four months of the year due to higher-than-expected demand.

    OpenAI has commenced construction on a 1-gigawatt data center in Michigan, a key component of its 'Stargate' initiative aimed at expanding AI infrastructure, creating jobs, and supporting local communities.
