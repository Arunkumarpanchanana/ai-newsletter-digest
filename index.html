Models & Research

  1. OpenAI Releases GPT-Realtime-2.1 and Mini for Low-Latency Voice Agents in API

    OpenAI has added two new Realtime models, GPT-Realtime-2.1 and GPT-Realtime-2.1-mini, to its API, designed to enhance low-latency voice agent capabilities, alongside a 25% reduction in p95 latency.

    Marktechpost·marktechpost.com ↗

  2. Tencent Unveils Hy3: An Open 295B Mixture-of-Experts Model with 256K Context Window

    Tencent's Hy team has released Hy3, a 295B Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) model with 21B active parameters and a 256K context window, available under the Apache 2.0 license for reasoning and long-context tasks.

    Marktechpost·marktechpost.com ↗

  3. Google Introduces Gemma 4: New Generation of Multimodal Open-Weight Language Models

    Google has introduced Gemma 4, a new family of open-weight, natively multimodal language models ranging from 2.3B to 31B parameters, designed for improved compute efficiency and reasoning.

    arXiv cs.CL·arxiv.org ↗

  1. Microsoft Replaces OpenAI and Anthropic Models with Own MAI to Cut AI Costs

  2. OpenAI and Anthropic Offer Millions in Free Compute to Attract Startups

    OpenAI, Anthropic, and major cloud providers are engaged in a competitive race to attract startups by offering millions in free compute credits, with some individual offers topping $3 million.

    The Decoder·the-decoder.com ↗

  3. Insilico Medicine Advances AI-Discovered Drug for IPF to Phase III Trials

    Insilico Medicine is moving its AI-identified drug for idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) into Phase III human trials, marking a significant step for computational drug discovery.

    AI News·artificialintelligence-news.com ↗
