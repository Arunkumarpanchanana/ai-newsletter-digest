  1. Research Explains Why Larger Language Models Acquire Skills Smaller Ones Miss

    A new study reveals that smaller language models fail at rare tasks because frequent ones overwrite learned information, suggesting increasing task frequency in training data could be an alternative to scaling models.

  2. Perplexity Introduces 'Search as Code' Allowing AI Models to Dynamically Write Search Pipelines

    Perplexity's new 'Search as Code' architecture enables AI models to generate their own Python search routines, filtering and deduplicating within a sandbox, outperforming fixed APIs and reducing token costs.

    The Decoder·the-decoder.com ↗

  3. UIUC and Chroma Release Harness-1, a 20B Retrieval Subagent with Public Weights

    Harness-1, a 20B retrieval subagent developed by UIUC and Chroma, uses reinforcement learning within a stateful search harness to achieve high curated recall, with its weights and code now publicly available.
