    Google DeepMind has released Gemma 4 12B, an encoder-free multimodal model with native audio capabilities, specifically designed to run locally on laptops with 16GB of RAM under an Apache 2.0 license.

    Marktechpost·marktechpost.com ↗

    ChatGPT has rolled out a new memory system, internally called 'Dreaming,' designed to better remember user preferences and maintain context across multiple conversations, improving the relevance of interactions.
