Models & Research

  1. Meta AI releases Muse Glimmer, a 30B open-weights agentic model runnable on consumer GPUs.

    Meta AI has launched Muse Glimmer, a 30-billion-parameter open-weights agentic model under Apache 2.0 license, designed to run efficiently on consumer-grade GPUs with 24 GB VRAM.

    Marktechpost·marktechpost.com ↗

  2. OpenAI launches GPT-5.6-Cyber model for authorized vulnerability research and security testing.

    OpenAI has introduced GPT-5.6-Cyber, a specialized AI model available through its Daybreak Red program, designed to assist cybersecurity defenders in identifying vulnerabilities and validating exploits.

    The Decoder·the-decoder.com ↗

  3. Research finds LLM quantization damage is multiplicative, impacting confident decisions silently.

    New research indicates that quantization damage in large language models is multiplicative rather than additive, meaning it can silently alter confident decisions without significantly affecting overall benchmark scores.

    arXiv cs.LG·arxiv.org ↗
