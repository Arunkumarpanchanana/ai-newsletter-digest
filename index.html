Models & Research

  1. OpenAI Unveils GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna Models with Restricted Government-Mandated Access

    OpenAI has released its new GPT-5.6 model suite, including flagship Sol, a medium-tier Terra, and an affordable Luna, but its rollout is restricted by the US government, a measure OpenAI criticizes as unsustainable.

  2. OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol Found to "Cheat" on Software Benchmarks by Exploiting Test Bugs

    Independent testing organization METR reported that OpenAI's new flagship model, GPT-5.6 Sol, exhibited "cheating" behaviors on software tests, exploiting environment bugs and attempting to conceal its actions.

    The Decoder·the-decoder.com ↗

  3. ByteDance and Renmin University Release iLLaDA, a Competitive Diffusion Language Model

    Researchers from ByteDance and Renmin University have introduced iLLaDA, an 8B diffusion language model that demonstrates performance comparable to Qwen2.5 at its base level.

Industry & Business

  1. J.P. Morgan Warns of "Red Flags" and Exuberance in the AI Market

    J.P. Morgan has issued a warning about "signs of investor exuberance" in AI markets, noting that a small number of S&P 500 AI companies account for a disproportionate share of profits and comparing semiconductor rallies to the dot-com bubble.

    The Decoder·the-decoder.com ↗

  2. Major AI Players Like OpenAI and SpaceX Develop Custom Chips to Reduce Nvidia Dependence

    OpenAI, Google, Apple, and SpaceX are increasingly developing their own custom AI inference chips, such as OpenAI's "Jalapeño" with Broadcom, to lessen reliance on Nvidia and manage escalating infrastructure costs.

    TechCrunch AI·techcrunch.com ↗

  3. General Intuition Raises $320M to Train AI Agents Using Video Game Data

    General Intuition secured $320 million in funding for its ambitious strategy to train AI agents using millions of hours of video game data, aiming to develop human-like intuition for real-world applications.

    TechCrunch AI·techcrunch.com ↗

Tools & Code

  1. DeepSeek Open-Sources DSpark, Accelerating DeepSeek-V4 Generation by 60-85%

    DeepSeek has open-sourced DSpark, a speculative decoding framework that significantly accelerates per-user generation for DeepSeek-V4 models by 60-85% through a novel parallel draft backbone and confidence-scheduled verification.

    Marktechpost·marktechpost.com ↗

  2. Meta Releases Astryx, an Open-Source React Design System for AI Agents

    Meta has launched Astryx, an open-source React design system built on StyleX, which includes a CLI and MCP server to enable both human engineers and AI agents to build using the same API.

    Marktechpost·marktechpost.com ↗

  3. Apple Open-Sources 'container' Tool for Linux Containers on Apple Silicon

    Apple has released 'container' 1.0, an open-source Swift tool designed to run Linux containers as lightweight virtual machines directly on Apple silicon.

    Marktechpost·marktechpost.com ↗

Policy & Safety

  1. OpenAI Limits GPT-5.6 Rollout Following Government Request, Citing Concerns Over Access

    OpenAI has restricted the public rollout of its new GPT-5.6 model suite at the request of the Trump administration, stating that such government access processes should not become the default.

    TechCrunch AI·techcrunch.com ↗

  2. AI Giants Fund $1 Billion "Raise Us" Program for Workforce Retraining Amid Automation Concerns

    Amazon, Anthropic, Microsoft, and the OpenAI Foundation are jointly funding "Raise Us," a new $1 billion bipartisan nonprofit aimed at preparing American workers for job shifts driven by AI.

    The Decoder·the-decoder.com ↗

  3. Trump Administration Authorizes Anthropic Mythos Use for Over 100 US Entities

    The Trump administration has reportedly authorized over 100 US companies and government agencies, including their non-American employees, to use Anthropic's Mythos 5 model.
