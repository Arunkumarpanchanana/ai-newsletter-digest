Models & Research

  1. Google DeepMind unveils Gemini 3.8 Flash and a specialized Cyber variant for governments and enterprises.

    Google DeepMind has released Gemini 3.8 Flash, its latest cost-optimized model, alongside Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber, a variant designed for proactive cyber defense with restricted access.

    Marktechpost·marktechpost.com ↗

  2. Anthropic releases Claude Fable 5.1, offering enhanced performance and up to 45% cost reduction for agentic work.

    Anthropic's new Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1 models promise stronger performance in coding and research, with Fable 5.1 specifically reducing costs for complex agentic tasks by up to 45%.

    The Verge AI·theverge.com ↗

  3. World Labs introduces Atlas, a unified AI model capable of generating, reconstructing, and simulating 3D worlds from minimal photos.

    Co-founded by Fei-Fei Li, World Labs announced Atlas, a novel world model that can create, reconstruct, and simulate complex 3D scenes using only a few input images, outperforming specialized models.

Industry & Business

  1. OpenAI's ChatGPT Ads platform achieves $1 billion annualized revenue run rate within 200 days of launch.

    OpenAI announced that its ChatGPT Ads platform has rapidly reached a $1 billion annualized revenue run rate and is expanding globally, offering self-service access to advertisers.

    OpenAI·openai.com ↗

  2. Palo Alto Networks acquires AI IT service automation startup Console for an estimated $500 million.

    Cybersecurity giant Palo Alto Networks has reportedly acquired Console, a Thrive-backed startup specializing in AI-driven IT service automation, for approximately $500 million.

    TechCrunch AI·techcrunch.com ↗

  3. US military integrates OpenAI's ChatGPT and xAI's Grok into its GenAI.mil platform.

    The Pentagon is expanding its secure AI platform, GenAI.mil, by incorporating OpenAI's ChatGPT Mil and xAI's Grok for Government, providing advanced AI capabilities to military personnel.

    The Decoder·the-decoder.com ↗

Tools & Code

  1. Google launches "Google Pics," an AI-first image creation and editing tool for Workspace.

    Google has launched Google Pics, a new AI-powered tool built on its Nano Banana model, designed for easy image creation and editing through natural language prompts within Google Workspace.

    TechCrunch AI·techcrunch.com ↗

  2. Perplexity introduces Hybrid Compute for Mac, enabling cloud AI agents to orchestrate tasks using local models for private data.

    Perplexity's new Hybrid Compute feature for Mac splits AI tasks between cloud-based frontier models and on-device local models, allowing agents to process sensitive data without sending it to the cloud.

    Marktechpost·marktechpost.com ↗

  3. NVIDIA open-sources Switchyard, a Rust proxy and library for routing and translating LLM traffic across APIs.

    NVIDIA has released Switchyard, an Apache-2.0 licensed Rust proxy and library designed to decode, route, and translate LLM requests and responses across different API providers like OpenAI and Anthropic.

    Marktechpost·marktechpost.com ↗

  1. OpenAI sued for "aiding and abetting" in Tumbler Ridge mass shooting, facing 30 new lawsuits.

  2. AI safety experts voice alarm over OpenAI's upcoming Astra model, citing potential security risks.

    Researchers are warning of potential safety disasters ahead of OpenAI's Astra model release, with concerns that its advanced reasoning techniques could pose unprecedented security and safety challenges.

  3. Trump administration backs OpenAI in NYT copyright lawsuit, arguing for fair use in AI model training.
