  1. Google DeepMind unveils new Gemini Flash models for enterprise agents, focusing on efficiency

  2. Black Forest Labs' Flux 3 model generates video with native audio, setting new benchmark

Industry & Business

  1. AMD commits up to $5 billion to Anthropic in major AI infrastructure partnership

    AMD will invest up to $5 billion in Anthropic, providing the AI company with up to two gigawatts of Instinct MI450 AI GPUs and Helios rack-scale systems to expand its computing power.

    AI News·artificialintelligence-news.com ↗

  2. Google reports first negative cash flow quarter ever, attributed to massive AI infrastructure spending

    Google experienced its first negative cash flow quarter, with its substantial investments in AI infrastructure and development significantly impacting its financial performance despite strong revenue.

    Ars Technica AI·arstechnica.com ↗

  3. OpenAI's Project Camellia secures 3.2-gigawatt power deal for Georgia data center

    OpenAI's Project Camellia in Effingham County, Georgia, has secured a 3.2-gigawatt power deal through 2032 for a new data center, alongside commitments for community investment and job creation.

Tools & Code

  1. Andrew Ng launches OpenWorker, an open-source, local-first desktop AI agent for deliverables

    Andrew Ng has released OpenWorker, an MIT-licensed desktop AI agent designed to produce finished deliverables rather than chat replies, supporting local and cloud-based tool-calling models.

    Marktechpost·marktechpost.com ↗

  2. Runway introduces AI Model Router to optimize generative media creation workflows

    Runway has launched the Media Router, a tool that automatically selects the optimal image, video, or audio generation model based on a developer's priorities for quality, speed, or cost.

    TechCrunch AI·techcrunch.com ↗

  3. Anthropic releases Claude Security plugin for Claude Code, a multi-agent vulnerability scanner

    Anthropic has launched the Claude Security plugin in beta for Claude Code, offering a multi-agent vulnerability scanner that operates within the terminal to identify and generate patches for code vulnerabilities.

    Marktechpost·marktechpost.com ↗
