Models & Research

  1. Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 Shows Marginal Performance Gain at Double the Cost

    Anthropic's new Claude Fable 5 model achieved a 5.7 percent performance increase over its predecessor, Opus 4.8, but comes with double the token price, according to an Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. The model sets records in five of ten benchmarks, yet safety filters with fallback routing further increase operational costs.

    The Decoder·the-decoder.com ↗

  2. Google AI Releases DiffusionGemma, an Open MoE Model for 4x Faster Text Generation

    Google DeepMind has released DiffusionGemma, an experimental 26B Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) open model that utilizes text diffusion to achieve up to four times faster generation speeds on GPUs. This Apache 2.0 licensed model represents a significant advance in efficient text generation.

    Marktechpost·marktechpost.com ↗

  3. Google's Gemini-SQL2 Achieves 80.04% Text-to-SQL Accuracy on BIRD Leaderboard

    Google Research announced Gemini-SQL2, a new text-to-SQL capability powered by Gemini 3.1 Pro, which scored an 80.04% execution accuracy on the BIRD single-model leaderboard. This advancement allows the model to accurately convert natural language queries into SQL commands.

    Marktechpost·marktechpost.com ↗

Industry & Business

  1. Jeff Bezos' AI Startup Prometheus Secures $12 Billion Funding at $41 Billion Valuation

    Jeff Bezos' AI startup, Prometheus, has successfully closed a $12 billion funding round, pushing its valuation to an impressive $41 billion, just months after its initial seed funding. The company, which launched last November, has not yet revealed specific products, with Bezos stating it would be "premature."

    The Decoder·the-decoder.com ↗

  2. SpaceX Officially Prices Shares at $135 in the Largest IPO in History

    SpaceX has officially priced its shares at $135, initiating what is now considered the largest Initial Public Offering (IPO) ever, marking a significant milestone for the combined rocket, AI, and social media company. This public debut allows investors to buy shares for the first time.

    TechCrunch AI·techcrunch.com ↗

  3. Mistral AI Reportedly Seeks €3 Billion Funding at €20 Billion Valuation

    French AI startup Mistral AI is rumored to be negotiating a new funding round of approximately €3 billion, which would value the company at around €20 billion ($23.15 billion). This potential valuation is nearly double its previous Series C valuation.

    TechCrunch AI·techcrunch.com ↗

Tools & Code

  1. OpenAI Acquires Ona to Advance Autonomous Coding Agents for Codex

    OpenAI has acquired Ona, a startup specializing in AI agents and secure cloud development environments, to enhance its Codex coding agent for long-running and autonomous coding tasks. Ona, formerly Gitpod, was founded in Kiel, Germany, in 2020.

    The Decoder·the-decoder.com ↗

  2. Moonshot AI Launches Kimi Work, a Local Desktop Agent with 300-Sub-Agent Swarm

    Moonshot AI has launched Kimi Work, a local desktop agent for macOS and Windows, reportedly running on Kimi K2.6 and featuring a 300-sub-agent swarm. The agent is designed to drive logged-in browsers via WebBridge and schedule background jobs autonomously.

    Marktechpost·marktechpost.com ↗

  3. Arbor Introduces Tree Search as Cognition Layer for Autonomous AI Agents

    Arbor is a new multi-agent framework that integrates structured tree search as a cognition layer for autonomous agents operating in large, stateful action spaces. Unlike prior systems, Arbor maintains an explicit search tree of scored hypotheses as shared working memory, evolving with measurements and treating failures as diagnostic signals.

    arXiv cs.AI·arxiv.org ↗

Policy & Safety

  1. US Government Directs Suspension of Access to Anthropic's Fable 5 and Mythos 5 Models

    The US government has issued an export control directive, citing national security authorities, to suspend all access to Anthropic's Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models for any foreign national, including Anthropic employees, both inside and outside the United States. Anthropic confirmed they must abruptly disable access for all affected customers.

    Simon Willison·simonwillison.net ↗

  2. Google Sues Chinese AI Scam Network with FBI, OpenAI Blocks PRC Influence Operations

    Google has filed its first joint lawsuit with the FBI against a Chinese cybercrime network that used Gemini to automate scams, while OpenAI simultaneously blocked operations allegedly originating in China that leveraged AI for fraud and covert influence campaigns. Both actions target US infrastructure and political debates.

    The Decoder·the-decoder.com ↗

  3. Google DeepMind Funds Research into Multi-Agent AI Safety Risks

    Google DeepMind and its partners are investing $10 million into research on the potential dangers arising from situations where millions of AI agents interact with each other online. The initiative aims to understand and mitigate risks associated with the mass-market deployment of autonomous AI agents.

    MIT Tech Review·technologyreview.com ↗
