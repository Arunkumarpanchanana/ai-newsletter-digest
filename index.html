  2. Alibaba's Wan3.0 AI model generates videos up to 30 seconds from text, images, and documents

    Alibaba has introduced Wan3.0, a new video generation model capable of creating 30-second clips from various inputs like text, PDFs, and PowerPoint files, with 1080p clips costing $6.

Industry & Business

  1. Hugging Face reportedly in talks for a $13 billion acquisition, founders weigh community responsibility

    Hugging Face, a central platform for AI models and datasets, is reportedly considering acquisition offers that could value the company at around $13 billion, though founders are deliberating their responsibility to the community.

    TechCrunch AI·techcrunch.com ↗

  2. Nvidia reportedly in talks to invest in Perplexity at a valuation exceeding $30 billion

    Nvidia is reportedly negotiating an investment in AI search startup Perplexity at a valuation over $30 billion, a significant increase from its last funding round, as Perplexity's annualized revenue triples.

    The Decoder·the-decoder.com ↗

  3. Thomson Reuters invests $40M to build its own AI, opting against renting from OpenAI or Anthropic

    Thomson Reuters is launching its proprietary language model, 'Thomson,' built on Alibaba's Qwen, with a $40 million investment over two years, aiming to own its AI capabilities rather than relying on third-party providers.

    The Decoder·the-decoder.com ↗

Tools & Code

  1. NVIDIA releases NeMo Guardrails guide for enterprise AI safety, moving beyond simple prompt filtering

    A developer's guide to NVIDIA's NeMo Guardrails framework demonstrates how to implement production-grade safety for LLM applications, including PII redaction, retrieval filtering, and policy-based tool gating.

    Marktechpost·marktechpost.com ↗

  2. SDAD framework formalizes Spec-Driven Agentic Development for AI-native software lifecycle

    A new report formalizes Spec-Driven Agentic Development (SDAD), a framework that integrates disciplined formal specification with high-context, multi-step reasoning AI coding agents to restructure the software development lifecycle.

    arXiv cs.AI·arxiv.org ↗

  3. SAGE introduces unified algebra for AI functions in SQL, enabling self-adaptive execution

    SAGE (Self-Adaptive Generative Execution) is a new framework that unifies AI functions like classification, extraction, and summarization into three relational roles within SQL, allowing natural composition with standard relational operations.

    arXiv cs.AI·arxiv.org ↗
