  1. Nemotron 3 Ultra: NVIDIA unveils 550B parameter hybrid MoE model for agentic reasoning

    NVIDIA introduced Nemotron 3 Ultra, a 550 billion total parameter Mixture-of-Experts hybrid Mamba-Transformer model designed for advanced agentic reasoning after extensive pre-training and post-training.

    arXiv cs.CL·arxiv.org ↗

  2. OpenAI introduces Deployment Simulation to predict AI model behavior for improved safety

    OpenAI has developed Deployment Simulation, a new method that uses real conversation data to predict AI model behavior before public release, aiming to enhance safety and evaluation accuracy.

    OpenAI·openai.com ↗

  3. "The AI Scientist" demonstrates end-to-end automation of AI research lifecycle

    Researchers have presented "The AI Scientist," a system capable of autonomously navigating the entire AI research lifecycle from idea generation to publication, including coding, experimentation, and data analysis.

    arXiv cs.AI·arxiv.org ↗

  2. Leaked documents reveal OpenAI is losing billions annually despite growing revenue

    Audited financial documents indicate that OpenAI incurred billions of dollars in losses last year, with rapidly increasing revenues being outpaced by substantial research and development and other operational expenses.

  3. Chinese AI startup DeepSeek raises $7.4 billion in first external funding round, valued at $50 billion

    Chinese AI startup DeepSeek secured over $7.4 billion in its inaugural external funding round, achieving a valuation of $50 billion, marking a significant milestone for the company.

Tools & Code

  1. Google Cloud introduces Open Knowledge Format (OKF) for AI agent context

    Google Cloud launched the Open Knowledge Format (OKF), a vendor-neutral Markdown specification designed to provide AI agents with curated context, formalizing the LLM-wiki pattern for structured information.

    Marktechpost·marktechpost.com ↗

  2. Atoms introduces "vibe coding" tool using AI agents for no-code app development

    Atoms unveiled a "vibe coding" tool that leverages AI agents to enable users to build, deploy, and market applications without writing code, by simply describing their ideas in natural language.

    Marktechpost·marktechpost.com ↗

  3. Android 17 launches with new multitasking and expanded Google Gemini AI features

    Google released Android 17 and Wear OS 7, introducing new multitasking tools, parental controls, and security features, alongside a Pixel Drop that integrates Google's latest AI models into its devices.

    TechCrunch AI·techcrunch.com ↗
